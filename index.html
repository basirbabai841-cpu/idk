Common questions

Does treatment hurt?

We use gentle numbing gel and modern anaesthetic. Most patients tell us it was easier than expected, and you can raise a hand to pause at any time.

How often should I visit?

Most people do well with a check-up every six months. We will recommend a schedule that fits your own needs.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Larger treatments can be split into interest-free monthly payments.

Can I bring my children?

Of course. We see children from their first tooth and keep family appointments together where we can.
